About

I am a PhD student at the University of Padova, advised by Prof. Fabio Vandin. I work on randomized approximation algorithms with provable guarantees for large-scale network analysis.

My current research studies the efficiency of Monte Carlo estimators under a finite time budget, with experiments on real-world graphs. I recently started to work on centrality measures in hypergraphs. More broadly, I am interested in sampling, variance reduction, statistical learning tools for rigorous approximation guarantees, and data mining.

I am looking for a 6-month research or software engineering internship starting around September 2027. Feel free to get in touch.
